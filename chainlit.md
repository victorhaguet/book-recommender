# Book RAG 📚

Welcome to **Book RAG**, a recommendation app built to match readers with books that fit what they are actually looking for.

This project is powered by the [7K Books with Metadata dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dylanjcastillo/7k-books-with-metadata) and uses retrieval-augmented generation to turn a natural-language request into one or several book suggestions.

## What You Can Ask

Try prompts like:

- "I want a fantasy story with wizards and political intrigue."
- "Recommend a moving historical novel with a strong female lead."
- "I am looking for a short mystery with a dark atmosphere."
- "Suggest a sci-fi book about survival and isolation."

## Why This App Exists

One strong use case is for **publishers and editors** who want to promote books from their own catalog.

Instead of always surfacing the most famous mainstream answer, a system like this can be tuned to recommend titles that match the reader's intent while also highlighting books the editor wants to push forward.

For example:

If a reader asks for *a story with wizards*, the goal is not automatically to answer *Harry Potter*, but to surface a relevant title from the publisher's own list that genuinely fits the request.

## How To Use It

Describe the kind of story, themes, atmosphere, genre, or character dynamics you want, and the app will return matching book recommendations.

The more specific your request is, the more precise the suggestions tend to be but higher is the risk of not having any good feat in the database.
